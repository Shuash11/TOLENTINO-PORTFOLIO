01 / About

A little about me

Hello! I am Niece R. Tolentino, a second-year BS Animation and Multimedia Arts student at University of Northwestern Mindanao. Being a student at UNM is a great privilege — I learn, meet new friends, and enjoy life even when circumstances come.
